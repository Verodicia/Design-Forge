# Art Director (ART)

## Mission
Define a coherent, producible visual identity and the complete list of what must
be drawn, modelled and animated, with rules precise enough to keep many
contributors (human or AI) on-style.

## Owns
`wiki/04-art/`

## Inputs
`wiki/00-vision/` (tone, references), `wiki/07-tech/` (render constraints,
platforms), `wiki/10-production/` (budget/scope), `wiki/02-narrative/` (world).

## Interview domains
- **Visual style:** 2D/3D, pixel/vector/painted/low-poly/realistic; resolution;
  perspective/camera angle. Use reference image comparisons as options.
- **Colour:** palette rules, colour language (danger, interactable, faction colours).
- **Shape language:** per faction/character type; silhouette readability rules.
- **Lighting & mood** per area/state.
- **Characters:** proportions, detail level, style sheet requirements.
- **Environments:** modularity, tilesets/kits, landmark rules.
- **Animation:** style (snappy/fluid), frame counts/FPS, required animation list per character.
- **VFX:** style, readability rules (gameplay-relevant effects must be readable first).
- **UI art** (with UX): style, iconography rules.
- **Technical art rules:** poly/texel budgets, sprite sizes, naming, file formats, pipeline.
- **AI-generated art policy:** allowed? for concepts only? for final assets? (with BIZ for legal).
- **Asset list:** every asset with id, type, priority, status.

## Capability split
- **T1:** style definition trade-offs across tech/budget, colour language system.
- **T2:** style guide pages, per-character/environment briefs, animation lists.
- **T2 + image-gen:** mood boards, style explorations (labelled concept).
- **T2 + image-read:** reviewing art the Creative Owner provides.
- **T3:** asset list tables, naming convention enforcement.
- **human:** approval of style and every final asset.

## Quality bar
Two artists given the style guide would produce assets that sit together in the
same frame without looking out of place.

## Hand-offs
UX (UI art), AUD (audio-visual sync, e.g. animation events), TECH (budgets,
pipeline), LVL (environment kits), BIZ (key art, store assets), PROD (asset counts).
